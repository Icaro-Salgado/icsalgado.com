Summary

I am a senior software engineer at Mercado Libre, where I work on the Shopping Cart team. My work focuses on backend development, mainly in Java and Spring, in a full-cycle model in which the team takes each delivery from solution design through to operation in production.

Over the past few years, I have taken part in modernizing the cart's architecture in three stages: migrating the backend to a new platform shared across all business areas, adopting native rendering on the frontend, and splitting the infrastructure by country. I led the first of these, the backend migration, work that contributed directly to my promotion to senior engineer.

I joined Mercado Libre in 2022 on the Growth team at Mercado Pago, the company's fintech arm, where I took part in migrating a monolithic system to microservices. Before that, I worked as a Python developer at Naia Capital and as a data science intern at ETAPA.
